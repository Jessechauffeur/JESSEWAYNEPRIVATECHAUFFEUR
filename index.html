Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Where do you provide chauffeur services?

We are based in Nanyuki and welcome enquiries for journeys around Kenya. Send your route so availability can be discussed.

Can you drive my own car?

You can enquire about hiring a chauffeur to drive your own vehicle. Share the trip details to discuss the arrangement.

Do you offer airport transfers?

You can enquire about airport pickups and drop-offs. Include the airport, flight information, pickup point and timing.

Can I book a long-distance trip?

Yes, you may send an enquiry for a long-distance trip. The route, date and availability must be discussed before confirmation.

Do you offer personal driver services?

Personal driver arrangements are available to enquire about. Share the schedule and duration you have in mind.

How do I make a booking?

Use the booking form to prepare a WhatsApp enquiry, or contact us directly. Your booking is not confirmed until details are agreed.

Do you provide taxi services?

Taxi enquiries are handled separately from the main chauffeur service. KCX 469B is shown in the taxi section at the end of the fleet listing.

How much does a trip cost?

Send your pickup, destination, date, time and service type to request a discussion about the trip and its cost.

Can I book from outside Kenya?

You can contact us online before travelling to Kenya. Include your planned dates and route so arrangements can be discussed.

What if my plans change?

Please contact us as soon as possible to discuss changes. Any change depends on the arrangements agreed for your booking.
